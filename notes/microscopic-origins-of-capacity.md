# Microscopic origins of capacity

Date: 16 October 2026

## Open-channel model
Finite number of transmissive microchannels per shell. Omega proportional to remaining open channels to a power yields the logarithmic barrier. Recovers Candidate C.

## Status
Motivated toy counting argument. The finite channel count is not forced by the locked laws. Other mechanisms (finite occupancy, capacitor-like budget) produce equivalent barriers. Membership in the universality class is the structural claim; the exact micro-mechanism remains open.
