# Lattice and defects

Date: 12 October 2026

Constitution first. Equal active and pause. On the deep voice the speed increases. On the soft voice the speed is held. Acceleration is the field. The tools track the midpoint identity and the integral.

## Lattice transmission

The layered rule N_k = k/(k+xi) gives the exact product

T_n = Gamma(1+xi) Gamma(n+1) / Gamma(n+1+xi).

For large n this is power-law, T_n ~ n^{-xi}. The cumulative delay is logarithmic. This is exact for finite stacks. It is bookkeeping of the product, not a new selection.

## One defect

A single defective shell at m with local offset delta multiplies the total transmission by (m+xi)/(m+xi+delta). The extra delay is C ln(1 + delta/(m+xi)). Defects change the prefactor, not the bulk exponent. Early defects matter more.

## Inverse

Total transmission and total delay alone recover only the ratio delta/(m+xi). They do not separate m from delta. A partial-stack scan does: the first shell where the ratio drops from 1 locates m, and the plateau size gives delta. Frequency dependence recovers the shape of a defect spectrum exactly, and the location only if the amplitude scale is known independently.

## Telescoping

The lapse increments telescope. The natural optical index is 1/N and gives the half-GR bending. Doubling each shell increment is an ansatz. It is not forced by the midpoint identity or the integral. It yields the full GR coefficient only if that ansatz is supplied.

The modular tool is unchanged. The two readings and the alpha dial remain bookkeeping. The lattice product, the defect ratio, and the telescoping half-branch are exact once the shell rule is chosen. The doubled branch and the spectral location remain conditional.
