# Constitution versus constitutive law

Date: 16 October 2026

## What the six laws determine
Kinematics: additive variable u = -ln T, shell composition, reciprocal closure, one-end regularity, equal active/pause, acceleration = field. Admissible profiles once T is given.

## What they do not determine
The local constitutive potential V(u). Geometry and polynomial stiffening do not produce a jammed plateau. Finite capacity is an additional axiom.

## Separation
Layer 1: locked kinematic constitution.
Layer 2: effective selector (nearest-neighbor energy).
Layer 3: constitutive capacity axiom (barrier class).
