# Two gaps

Date: 10 October 2026

Constitution first. The pause holds a speed. The held energy at low store is (1/2) m sigma^2. Capacity is room left before a threshold. The cone spreads the field. The feed is local at the source.

## The coefficient test fails

A retarded scalar potential, expanded for a slow source, cancels its linear velocity term. The leading correction is quadratic and two-body: proportional to q1 q2 (n-hat · v)^2 / (w^2 R). The relativistic one-body term is (1/8) m sdot^4 / w^2. It exists for a free particle with no source. Different order, different body count. A retarded cone does not reproduce it. w is not forced. Retardation is insufficient, not a trap.

The previous note fused the field's propagation speed with the store's energy function. That fusion holds only if all inertia is field energy. The constitution puts mass m in the store directly. The two gaps stay two.

## Where the one-body term lives

It lives in E(sigma) near capacity.

Relativistic, limiting speed w: the next term after (1/2) m sigma^2 is + (1/8) m sigma^4 / w^2.
Saturating, finite cap: the next term is negative, for example - (1/2) m sigma^4 / sigma_max^2.

Opposite signs. A hard capacity reads as saturating and rejects the relativistic form. An infinite-energy wall, E growing without bound as sigma approaches w, is the relativistic form. The constitution's capacity, as room before a threshold, is the hard-cap reading. It does not yet supply the wall.

## The number

xi/T = 0.0113. Sigma = 0.07084. Their ratio is 2 pi exactly, because sigma = Delta E and xi/T = Delta E / (2 pi) at d = 2, v = 1. The held speed exceeds the local ratio. If xi/T were the invariant, the store would be superluminal. It cannot be. Any invariant must exceed the largest store, and the only computed speed in the notes is smaller.

## What is asked

Not another retarded expansion. One question about the store: is E(sigma) bounded, or does it rise without bound as sigma approaches a wall? Bounded keeps the aether reading. Unbounded is the kinematic seed of a limiting speed. The field's retardation is downstream of that answer.
