# Triangle layer

Date: 12 October 2026

Constitution first. Volume is exact and unchanging. Circumference is fixed. The funnel stretches. Equal active and pause. Acceleration is the field. The tools track the midpoint identity and the integral of the acceleration.

## What the triangle is

The assignment pause = rest leg, active = momentum leg, hypotenuse = total is a coherent re-description of the phases under the special closure lambda = 1 and xi = R0. The proper-time fraction is the product of the lapse and the kinematic cosine. That is the equivalence principle restated. The geometry is right-triangular only at that same closure. The spatial coupling, if any, is to the momentum leg, not the total.

## What it does not do

It does not change the midpoint identity. It does not change the integral that tracks the speed change. It does not select the alpha dial. It does not resolve the volume-circumference conflict. It is not a third version of the tool. It is an interpretive layer that sits on the special closure and re-labels the phases.

## Advice

Keep the modular tool as it is. The two variations and the alpha dial remain the bookkeeping. The triangle can be noted as a possible re-description under the special closure. It does not replace or extend the tracking. Use it only if a later derivation forces the closure; until then it is optional language.
