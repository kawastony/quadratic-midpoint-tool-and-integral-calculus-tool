# Gravity constitution on the three bridges

Date: 9 October 2026

Bridges from kawastony/grok-notes-version-3, notes/01_three_bridge_minimal_model.md and notes/00_constitution_v3.md. The story is the constitution. Active acceleration is gravity.

| Bridge | Note | Constitution | Acceleration | Speed | Observer |
|--------|------|--------------|--------------|-------|----------|
| A | Active span, deep, membership locked | Deep voice | a = g | Increases by g tau | Cannot reassign membership while this bridge speaks |
| P_1 | Transitional pause, mid, membership soft | Soft voice, feels safe | a = 0 | v+ = v- | May advise; does not change v or g |
| P_2 | Stalled / hollow pause, shallow, exit needs high alpha | The trap | a = 0 | v+ = v- | Acceptance is alpha. High alpha opens the exit. v and g unchanged |

Equal intervals: A and P_1 share duration tau. P_2 is the stall inside the pause, not a third equal voice.

After n deep voices on A, with each P_1 holding the speed,

v_n = n g tau.

Leaving P_2 does not alter that value. The worked table at g = 9.81 m/s^2 and tau = 1 s still ends at 29.43 m/s and 103.01 m whether or not alpha opens the exit.
