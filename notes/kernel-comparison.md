# Kernel comparison

Date: 17 October 2026

## Setup
Same two-parameter protocol. Logistic versus error-function switch.

## Findings
- Error-function kernel produces higher peak heights and narrower widths at low rates.
- Logistic kernel produces lower peaks and broader, sometimes non-monotonic widths.
- Both show peak-height decline with rate.
- Error-function width remains monotonic in the sampled range; logistic width does not.

## Correction
Earlier note that erf peaks are lower is reversed. Erf peaks are higher under the present normalization.

## Fusion mapping
The mismatch diagnostic can be formally translated (input power → φ, pressure gradient → Z). No L-H transition data were applied. The mapping remains a proposal.

## Status
Kernel choice changes peak height and width morphology. Peak-height decline is the stable feature.
