# Barrier universality of the jammed plateau

Date: 16 October 2026

## Statement
For any local constitutive potential V(u) defined on a finite domain 0 <= u < u_star with V'(u) -> +infinity as u -> u_star^-, the central response u_0 under arbitrarily large load remains bounded and approaches u_star from below.

## Argument
At the center the equilibrium condition reduces to V'(u_0) = J_0 + regular terms. If V' diverges at the boundary of the domain, then for any finite J_0 there exists u_0 < u_star satisfying the equation. As J_0 -> infinity, u_0 must approach u_star to keep V' finite. The approach rate depends on the strength of the divergence but the existence of the ceiling does not.

## Numerics
Log, power-alpha=1, and power-alpha=0.5 barriers all produce the ceiling. The gap size varies with the exponent. The plateau is universal; the quantitative approach is not.

## Status
Structural result. Holds for the capacity-limited class. Not derived from the locked laws.
