# Analogue systems

Date: 16 October 2026

## Granular jamming
Load = packing fraction or pressure. Response = coordination or strain. Capacity = finite contact network. Incremental rearrangement collapses above φc. Matches Candidate C.

## Traffic flow
Load = density. Response = flow or speed. Capacity = lane occupancy / jam density. Throughput collapses at jam density. Geometry of the road unchanged. Matches Candidate C.

## Porous media
Load = pressure gradient. Response = flux. Capacity = available pore pathways. Relative permeability collapses as saturation approaches residual. Matches Candidate C.

## Status
These systems realize the constitutive signature (finite local capacity, plateau) without reproducing the cone geometry. They test portability of the capacity axiom.
