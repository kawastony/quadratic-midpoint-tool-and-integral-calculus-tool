# Energy and branch

Date: 9 October 2026

Constitution first. The pause holds the speed. The deep voice is what filled it. The integral tool gives the held speed, sigma = n Delta v.

## Energy

The earlier E = (1/2) m g_0 sigma is retired. With g_0 = sigma / T that expression is a power, not an energy.

The pause holds a speed, so the energy it holds is the kinetic energy of that speed,

E = (1/2) m sigma^2.

Mass times speed squared is energy. At the locked point one deep voice holds sigma = 0.07084, so E = (1/2) m (0.07084)^2. Three deep voices hold nine times that, because sigma has tripled.

## Neighbor capacity

Each neighbor i has a free capacity C_i, in the same units as sigma. Handoff is allowed only if

sum_i C_i >= sigma_before.

Then sigma_parent = 0 and sigma_i = sigma_before * C_i / sum C_j, so the shares fill capacity in proportion and the sum is conserved. If the inequality fails, there is no handoff.

## Section choice

No handoff, and sigma > sigma_c: compare the two section energies at the same store. The strip spreads as 1/s, the solid cone as 1/s^2. The work to carry the store out to a fixed outer generator S is larger for the slower falloff, so

E_strip > E_solid

at the same sigma. The parent flips to the solid cone when that inequality holds and sigma has crossed sigma_c. It reverts to the strip only if sigma falls below sigma_r < sigma_c and E_strip becomes the lower store. The voices do not enter the comparison.
