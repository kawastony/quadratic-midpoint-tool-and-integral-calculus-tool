# Constitution

Date: 9 October 2026

The story is the constitution. The numbers 6 and 3 were guidance for the midpoint identity, not the values of the tool.

A person is standing. A deep voice speaks and he falls, faster and faster. A soft voice speaks and the falling stops: the increase stops, the speed is held, and it feels safe. After an equal interval the deep voice returns. The fall continues from the speed already reached, and it increases further. The soft voice returns. This repeats.

An independent observer advises him without affecting the dynamics. The advice is to accept his fate. Acceptance is the exit from the trap. The likeness is the equivalence principle: acceptance changes his relation to the motion, not the motion.

## Key

The acceleration during the deep-voice phase is gravity.

a_active = g
a_pause = 0

Equal durations. Speed is conserved across the pause. The observer does not change speed or g.

## What the tools must hold

- Midpoint: the person. Voices move. He does not.
- Deep voice: active phase, boundaries closing, a = g.
- Soft voice: pause, boundaries held, a = 0, v+ = v-.
- Integral: accumulated fall. It stacks because each pause carries the speed the last deep voice left.
- Observer: a variable that can open the exit and cannot change v or g.
