# Reduced feedback model results

Date: 17 October 2026

## Model
Turbulence T and shear S. Power P ramped. Turbulence grows with P and is suppressed by S. Shear is driven by turbulence and damped. Euler integration.

## Result
At intermediate rates an abrupt rise in shear occurs. The mismatch dS/dP peaks near the rise. Peak location and height vary with ramp rate. At the lowest rate no sustained shear develops. At the highest rate the final shear overshoots.

## Interpretation
The threshold is generated by the feedback, not imposed. The mismatch diagnostic tracks the emergent shear rise under continuous drive. The model is minimal and numerically sensitive.

## Status
Pipeline extended to a generated threshold. Not a validated L-H model. No real fusion data.
