# Granular fit plan

Date: 16 October 2026

## Dataset
O’Hern et al. (PRL 2002, PRE 2003) and Majmudar et al. photoelastic disks.

## Observable pair
- Load: packing fraction φ - φc
- Response: pressure P (continuous) and contact number Z (jump)

## Test
- Tier 1: does saturating u reproduce the continuous rise of P?
- Tier 2: does z crossing zc reproduce the jump in Z?

## Failure criterion
- If pressure itself jumps discontinuously, pure capacity is insufficient.
- If coordination remains continuous, locking variable is unnecessary.

## Status
First quantitative target for the two-tier split.
