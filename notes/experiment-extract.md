## Useful from the experiment

The clean spatial equation is a = c^2 d ln N/dr. With N = s/(s+xi) this is a = c^2 xi / (s(s+xi)), which falls as 1/s^2 at large s. That is already in the modular framework. The potential form Phi = c^2 ln N gives the same field. The divergence form is the source law. None of these need the cone.

A cone with linear R is intrinsically flat. It exerts no force. The force comes from the lapse. Coupling energy to space means breaking the special closure R = s/N, and that costs one parameter. The cone is an interpretation of lambda, not a replacement for the modular tool.

## Densification

The rate at which mass is densified is not a new equation. In the modular framework it is the divergence of the acceleration field. That is the source law, not a depletion of hope or a separate energy sink. Time slowing is the lapse. Gravity is the gradient of the lapse. The densification language maps to the divergence, nothing more.

## The two cone constitutions

Both readings keep the deep-end circumference fixed and the volume fixed, and allow elongation. In the water-flow reading the far end narrows. In the dispersing reading the far end opens. Those are the two orientations of the same one-end-fixed form. Making one cone fixed-circumference and the other fixed-volume was the parallel-readings move. It made the conflict visible as the alpha dial. It did not derive a connection between them. The tools track either reading once chosen. They do not choose it.

## What the framework is now

The modular tool remains the core. The two readings and the alpha dial are bookkeeping. The triangle is an optional re-description under the special closure. The cone is an optional geometric picture of lambda. The spatial force is the lapse gradient. Nothing in the experiment replaces the tool or resolves the volume-circumference conflict.
