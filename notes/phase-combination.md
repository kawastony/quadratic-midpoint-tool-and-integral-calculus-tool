# Phase combination

Date: 12 October 2026

Constitution first. Equal active and pause. On the deep voice the speed increases. On the soft voice the speed is held. Acceleration is the field. The tools track the midpoint identity and the integral of the acceleration.

## Additive

The integral sees only the active half. Pause contributes zero to the speed change. Per cycle the accumulated Delta v is the active contribution alone. That is additive bookkeeping. The recurring 1/2 is the duty cycle. The recurring 2 is the full cycle relative to one phase. Neither is forced by the midpoint identity.

## Multiplicative

The proper-time fraction is the product of the lapse and the kinematic cosine. Under free-fall that product is N squared. Under hover it is N. That is the equivalence principle restated. It is not an additive sum of the phases. It is a product of two factors that the constitution already separates.

## What the tools do not do

The quadratic midpoint identity is exact for any quadratic. It does not constrain how the phases combine. The integral tracks the speed change under the field. It sees only the active contribution. Neither tool selects additive or multiplicative. Both combinations are allowed once the reading is chosen.

## Advice

Keep both. Additive is the bookkeeping of the cycle. Multiplicative is the proper-time product under the special closure. Neither replaces the other. The telescoping derivation that doubles the shell increment is an ansatz. It is not forced by the tools. The recurring 1/2 and 2 are the duty cycle and its inverse. They are not a new selection principle.
