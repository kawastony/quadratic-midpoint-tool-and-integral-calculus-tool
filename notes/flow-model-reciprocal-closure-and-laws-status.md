# Flow-model derivation of reciprocal closure and laws status

Date: 18 October 2026

## Summary of attached derivations

The cone flow model (fixed volume, one-end fixed, fixed transit time) yields:

- Elongation at fixed volume narrows the pipe and raises mean flow speed: \(\delta \bar v / \bar v = \epsilon\).
- Lapse form \(N = s/(s+\xi)\) implies \(v = c\sqrt{1-N^2}\). Identifying \(\gamma = 1/N\) then gives \(g_{tt} = -N^2 = -1/\gamma^2\), \(g_{rr} = \gamma^2\), so \(g_{tt} g_{rr} = -1\).
- Reciprocal closure (Law 6) is therefore the metric expression of the Lorentz factor under this identification, not an independent postulate.
- Past the \(cT\) ceiling, additional mass loading is forced into the time sector (proper time freezes while coordinate transit grows). This is the mass-to-time coupling derived from the speed bound.
- Speed saturation is not mass saturation: flux \(\dot m \propto \rho\) can continue while \(v\) is capped.

Midpoint identity remains exact for quadratics; its remainder is proportional to the third derivative and functions as a breakpoint detector. Euler curvature is the complementary second-derivative measure. Interior-versus-edge filtering addresses terminal artifacts in the BETAN analysis.

## Status of the six laws

The original six laws remain the kinematic grammar. The flow derivation shows that Law 6 (reciprocal closure) follows from the tip-speed bound once \(N = 1/\gamma\) is adopted. Laws 1–5 are unchanged. No new law is required. The derivation re-expresses existing constraints in flow language; it does not replace the grammar.

## What is established versus open

- Established within the model: reciprocal closure as Lorentz reciprocity under the stated identification; mass-to-time residual channel past the speed ceiling; midpoint tool as breakpoint detector.
- Diagnostic only: BETAN slope/curvature stratification and interior filtering.
- Open: dynamical enforcement of \(v < c\) (horizonlessness as constraint); constitutive potential and matter coupling for a full field equation; quantitative link between plasma regime shifts and the geometric budget.

## Notes on attached manuscripts

The variational selector and composite regular-center profiles remain phenomenological. The flow derivation supplies one route by which reciprocal closure can be obtained from a speed-limited lapse; it does not derive the transmission profile \(\mathcal T(r)\) or the constitutive potential \(V(u)\) from the six laws alone.
