# Rate dependence results (synthetic)

Date: 16 October 2026

## Setup
Four rates: 10^{-5}, 10^{-4}, 10^{-3}, 10^{-2}. Jump replaced by logistic of width ~ rate. Apparent φc shifted linearly with rate.

## Findings
- Peak height of dZ/dφ falls as rate increases.
- Peak location moves slightly higher with rate.
- FWHM broadens at intermediate rates.

## Interpretation
Under this synthetic protocol the mismatch peak is rate-sensitive. The ideal singularity becomes a finite peak whose height and location depend on driving rate. Real data are required to test whether the same trend appears.
