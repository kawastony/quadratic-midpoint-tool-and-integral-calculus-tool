# Two-parameter rate trends

Date: 16 October 2026

## Model
Apparent onset: φc(r) = φ0 + A r^ν
Width: w(r) free (not forced to a single power).

## Synthetic findings
- Peak height of dZ/dφ falls monotonically with rate.
- Apparent onset shifts upward; rough ν ~ 0.27 on the lowest three rates.
- FWHM is non-monotonic; intermediate rate broadens most.

## Interpretation
A two-parameter description (shift + width) organizes the synthetic trends without requiring a master curve. The highest-rate point remains an outlier under pure power-law rescaling of height. Trends are the primary result.

## Status
Diagnostic pipeline records rate-sensitive onset features. Collapse is not claimed.
