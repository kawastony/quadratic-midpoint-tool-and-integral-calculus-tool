# u-z coupling

Date: 16 October 2026

## Meaning

u: capacity usage / filling.
z: network coordination / active constraints.
The coupling z(u) is the constitutive bridge from exhaustion to closure.

## Candidate forms

### UZ-1: smooth power law
z = z0 + a (u / u★)^m

### UZ-2: delayed onset
z = z0 for u < uc
z = z0 + a ((u - uc) / (u★ - uc))^m for u ≥ uc

### UZ-3: logistic onset
z = z0 + Δz / (1 + exp(-k (u - uc)))

## Interpretation
- a: strength of conversion from filling to coordination
- m: early vs late locking
- uc: occupancy threshold before network formation

## Status
Constitutive bridge. Not derived from locked laws or shell structure. Preserves the distinction between filling and locking. First recommended trial is UZ-2.
