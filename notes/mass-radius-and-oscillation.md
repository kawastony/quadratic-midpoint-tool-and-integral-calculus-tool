# Mass, radius, and the oscillation

Date: 9 October 2026

Gravity for a body is G M / r^2. The relevant packet pieces are the wall mass v and a radius. At the locked point v = 1, separation d = 2, decay length xi = 1/(2v) = 0.5, Delta E = 0.07084.

Two readings:

g = G v / d^2 = G/4

g = G v / xi^2 = 4 G

Both are the planetary formula with the packet's own mass and radius. G is the something else. The packet does not contain a kilogram or a metre, so these stay in packet units until a length is named. The form is the ordinary one.

The oscillation is the interchange, not a label added afterward. Speed v(t) = (d Delta E / 2) sin(Delta E t) rises from 0 to T/2 and falls from T/2 to T. The two halves are equal, 22.17 each. The rising half is the active phase: mass or liquid is moving toward the middle, speed increasing. The falling half is the pause side: the speed is coming back down. Current I(t) = (Delta E / 2) sin(Delta E t) is the flow. It peaks at the middle and integrates to one unit over the full clock.

On the elongating cone this is the pour. Active half: the funnel is feeding, flux rising. Pause-side half: the flux falls, speed is not being increased. The next cycle repeats the interchange. Acceptance still does not change v, d, or G.
