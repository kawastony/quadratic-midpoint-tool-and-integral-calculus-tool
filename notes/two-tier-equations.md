# Minimal two-tier equations

Date: 16 October 2026

## Tier 1: capacity

u(J0) = u★ (1 - exp(-J0 / Jc))
Smooth saturation. Incremental slope collapses. No jump.

## Tier 2: coordination
z(J0) = z0 + a (u / u★)^m

## Locking
R = H(z - zc)
R = 0 open, R = 1 locked.
zc = 2d for frictionless spheres.

## Regimes
- Open: z < zc
- Marginal: z ≈ zc
- Locked: z ≥ zc

## Observables
- u tracks continuous pressure-like response
- z tracks coordination
- R tracks rigidity onset

## Status
Toy prototype. Separates filling from locking. Does not yet derive z from shell structure.
