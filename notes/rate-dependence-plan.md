# Rate dependence plan

Date: 16 October 2026

## Goal
Test whether the onset peak in dZ/dφ broadens or shifts with compression rate.

## Observables
- Z(φ; r)
- M_Z = dZ/dφ
- peak location, height, FWHM

## Synthetic test
Ideal jump smeared by a logistic of width proportional to rate, with a small rate-dependent shift of φc.

## Status
Pipeline defined. Synthetic scan shows broadening and modest shift. Not yet applied to real multi-rate data.
