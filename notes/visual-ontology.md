# Visual ontology of the framework

Date: 16 October 2026

## Inward geometry (pre-saturation)
Elongating cone / inward funnel. Kinematic transmission under moderate load. Channels open, response still elastic. Corresponds to the active phase of the locked laws: equal intervals, acceleration equal to the field, fixed volume and one-end circumference.

## Outer shell (saturated)
Capacity-limited ring encasing the core. Finite glowing channels, some closed. Incremental response collapsed. The cone is not abolished; it is constrained by the constitutive barrier.

## Status
These are explanatory anchors, not evidence. The locked laws remain kinematic. The barrier remains an additional constitutive axiom. The pair maps the separation already established: geometry arranges the shells; finite local capacity decides whether they still transmit under extreme load.
