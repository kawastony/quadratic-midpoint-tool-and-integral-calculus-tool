# Phase and velocity check

Date: 16 October 2026

## Conserved quantities
Fixed volume and fixed open circumference. No additional topological invariant (winding number, genus, etc.) is generated by the kinematics.

## Active/pause vs tiers
Active phase = external elongation under load.
Pause phase = no external elongation.
These distinguish loading from rest. They do not distinguish filling (capacity usage) from locking (isostatic contact count). Locking is a network condition, not a pause.

## Constant elongation speed
External elongation rate can be held constant while the tip radius changes and internal speeds vary. This is a kinematic choice. It does not select Tier 1 or Tier 2. Acceleration inside the cone remains compatible with either continuous filling or a later locking event.

## Status
The two phases organize loading versus rest. They do not capture the filling/locking split. The constant external speed is not a tier identifier.
