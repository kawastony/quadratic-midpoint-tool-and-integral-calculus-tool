# Stretch anisotropy

Date: 11 October 2026

Constitution first. The funnel stretches. Volume is fixed. Circumference is fixed. Equal active and pause. On the deep voice the speed increases and that is the field. The clock is T = pi / sigma. Time slowed in the active phase is proportional to the gravity strength, as the inverse square root.

## What the stretch gives

Fixed circumference freezes the transverse radius. The only changing length is axial. During the active phase the funnel elongates by 50 percent, so the axial factor is 3/2. During the pause it is 1. A signal along the axis feels that factor. A signal around the circumference does not. The ratio of the two travel times is exactly the axial factor, 1 or 1.5, and it does not depend on whether the signal rides the flow or moves at a fixed free-stream speed. That is the one prediction free of the missing invariant speed.

The launch-averaged axial factor is 5/4. Averaging the squared component instead gives 13/8. They differ by 2 percent. The time integral uses the linear average.

## What is still a choice

The regularized profile sigma proportional to s/(s+xi) turns the 1/s clock into a log delay. Over a length xi, from xi to 2 xi, the free-stream ratio is 1.693 and the convected ratio is 2.887. The log coefficient is xi in one reading and 2 xi in the other. That profile is hosted, not selected. The notes do not fix which end is deep, so the sign of the slowing is open.

## Lapse

Force and clock are both powers of sigma, so T^2 g = pi. That is an identity of the constitution, not a new number. Reading sigma^2 as kinetic gives the 1/s^2 force and a power-law clock. Reading it as potential can give a 1/s clock and loses the force. The notes select the kinetic reading. The shared sigma is a lapse-like structure. It is not a spatial metric, and the fixed circumference gives no off-axis coupling, so there is no bending.

## What is not claimed

No lightcone is derived. No weak-field limit. The only length is xi. The anisotropy is the keeper: it is a ratio, it is constitutional, and it does not need the missing speed.
