# Discrete jump status

Date: 16 October 2026

## In the continuous capacity model
Candidate C produces smooth approach of u₀ to u★. No spontaneous discrete reorganization.

## In granular jamming
Contact number Z jumps discontinuously from 0 to the isostatic value Zc = 2d at the jamming point, then rises continuously as a power law above φc. Pressure rises continuously. The jump is in the order parameter of the contact network, not a first-order thermodynamic discontinuity in all observables.

## Status
A discrete onset exists in the contact-network order parameter of granular systems. The pure capacity law of Candidate C does not generate it. Bridging the continuous barrier to a discrete jump requires additional structure (constraint counting, isostaticity) not present in the current constitutive axiom.
