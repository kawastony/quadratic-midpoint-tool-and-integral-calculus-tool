# Stretch and clock

Date: 11 October 2026

Constitution first. Equal active and pause is the two-level half-period. On the deep voice the speed increases. The clock is T = pi / sigma. Time slowed in the active phase is proportional in some way to the gravity strength.

## What is forced

Constant volume, funnel elongating: r proportional to h to the -1/2, area proportional to 1/h. Incompressible flow then gives v proportional to h. A constant stretch rate gives a constant acceleration. Matching that acceleration to g = sigma / T forces the funnel to elongate by 50 percent per active phase. At the locked point, T = 44.35, active half = 22.17, speed rises from sigma to 1.5 sigma, and a = 0.001597 = g. The stretch fraction equals the locked radius fraction xi = 1/2.

The same flow sets the clock. T = pi / sigma and g = sigma^2 / pi together give T = sqrt(pi / g). Slowness scales as the inverse square root of g, not linearly and not as 1/g. That is the proportionality the constitution allows.

## What is not forced

Continuity on a surface gives sigma proportional to 1/s, so g proportional to 1/s^2. That is the Newtonian force. The clock then scales as s, linear, not as 1/s. A regularized profile sigma proportional to s/(s+xi) makes the period a bulk term plus xi/s, and both the force gate and a 1/s clock shift pass. That profile is hosted by the flow. It is not selected by it. The sign of the slowing depends on which end of the funnel is deep, and the notes do not fix that.

## What is not claimed

This is not a metric. A medium can also delay and shift a clock. The shared structure is that force and clock both read the same flow. That is the advance. The regularization is not yet required by the constitution.
