# Extraction and the local speed

Date: 10 October 2026

Constitution first. The deep voice increases speed. The soft voice holds it. The cone feeds outward from the source. The integral of the packet acceleration over a full clock is zero. The integral over the active half is Delta v = 0.07084.

## Extraction

The store is not in H_2. The packet returns its centroid to zero. A nonzero store is what left during the feed. The extraction splits in two.

(a) Why does anything leave? Because the store integrates only the outward half. The full-clock integral is zero, so a store that integrated the whole clock would be empty. The cone direction is the rectifier. This part is already in the sign seam.

(b) Why does all of it leave? Sigma equals Delta v exactly, 0.07084. A partial leak would be a fraction. The full value is what you get when the feed is on only during the active half and off during the return. That is a point coupling at the source, xi, not a feed spread out to R. The field spreads. The feed does not. The passive-half integral of the store is zero because lambda is off there. Sigma = Delta v is then the carrier's response to the active stroke, not a second typed fraction.

The lower threshold at xi, where the cost gap is zero, is degenerate. It means revert only at an empty store. A shallower loop would need a second geometric scale. None is in the cone yet.

## xi/T

The decay length is xi = 1/(2v) = 0.5. The clock is T = pi / Delta E = 44.35. Their ratio is

xi / T = 0.0113

in packet units. The length comes from the wall. The period comes from the two-level split. Neither is derived from the other, so the ratio is a real speed for this source. It is not an invariant. A different v or a different d changes Delta E and changes the ratio. The notes do not contain a prior locking of xi/T to a universal c. Computing it now does not add one.

## a and v

The constitution already sets the active acceleration equal to the field the receiver feels. That is a to g, as an effective identification. The held speed sigma, and Delta v, can be accumulated, handed off, and different per source. An invariant speed cannot. So v is not c. If a universal ratio enters later, it is a property of the cone map, not a quantity the store holds.
