# Field energy and the core

Date: 10 October 2026

Constitution first. The pause holds a speed. The deep voice fills it. The integral gives sigma. The held energy is (1/2) m sigma^2. Capacity C_i is the remaining room in a neighbor's held-speed store, in the same units as sigma: how much more speed it can absorb before its own upper threshold.

## Carrier cost

The cost of carrying the store from the source radius xi out to R is the potential drop, m times the integral of g(s).

Strip, g(s) = g_0 xi/s:
Delta Phi = g_0 xi ln(R/xi).

Solid cone, g(s) = g_0 (xi/s)^2:
Delta Phi = g_0 xi (1 - xi/R).

With g_0 = 0.00160 and xi = 0.5, at R = 4 xi the strip drop is 1.11e-3 and the solid drop is 5.99e-4. At R = 8 xi they are 1.66e-3 and 6.99e-4. The strip costs more once R/xi is past e. A g^2 measure gives the same order: from xi to 8 xi the strip proxy is 1.33e-6 and the solid proxy is 2.79e-7.

So E_strip > E_solid is a calculation, not a slogan, for an outer scale past e xi. The flip waits for sigma > sigma_c and for the solid cost to be lower by that integral, not by a tie. Reversion waits for sigma < sigma_r.

## Hand

The receiver action has m in both terms, (1/2) m sdot^2 - m Phi. That coupling is symmetric. It has no cross product and no hand. The rectifier lambda is a sign on the source, choosing the active half. It is not a hand on the receiver. Gravity in this model has no hand rule because the load is symmetric. The missing join named by the hand rule is this sign, and the constitution already is that sign. It is not a magnitude.

## What is not claimed

The engine that stores and rectifies is device-like. The spreading 1/s^2 is the Gauss law a cone shares with a point source. The load is the symmetric m. Gravitoelectromagnetism is weak-field gravity written in Maxwell form, and it needs a metric this model does not have. Kaluza-Klein derives the Maxwell piece from higher-dimensional gravity, the opposite arrow. Neither is reached here.
