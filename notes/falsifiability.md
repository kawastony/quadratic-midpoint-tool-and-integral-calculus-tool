Date: 16 October 2026

## What rules out Candidate C
- Incremental response never trends toward zero under monotone load.
- Measured ceiling is protocol-dependent and unstable.
- Transition is discontinuous and hysteretic in a manner C cannot reproduce.

## What shows an apparent plateau
- Slope decreases but continues to fall without a stable bound.
- Apparent saturation is only a slow stiffening (Candidate B).

## What forces a network/jump model
- Order parameter jumps discontinuously while pressure rises continuously (isostatic signature).
- Constraint count equals degrees of freedom at onset.

## Status
C is falsifiable on continuous saturation. The isostatic jump is a separate extension.
