# Exit laws

Date: 9 October 2026

Constitution first. The deep voice fills the store. The soft voice holds it. Equal intervals. Acceptance does not move the store. The integral tool is what fills it: sigma = n Delta v.

The stretch energy is the work stored against the source force,

E = (1/2) m g_0 sigma.

It is zero when the store is empty and it grows with sigma. The pause does not spend it.

## Conservation

On a redistribution the store is handed off, not created or destroyed.

sigma_parent + sum_i sigma_i = sigma_before.

After the spring-back, sigma_parent = 0 and each of N neighbors holds sigma_before / N. The field each neighbor emits is g_0,i = sigma_i / T.

## Threshold, with hysteresis

Two levels, the same pattern as the bridge leave and return. Disperse or evolve only if sigma > sigma_c. A cone that has changed section does not revert until sigma < sigma_r, with sigma_r < sigma_c. Between them the section stays as it is. Acceptance does not cross either level.

## Which exit

If surrounding cones can take a share, the exit is dispersion and the parent resets. If they cannot, the exit is evolution: sigma stays on the parent, and the section index flips when sigma crosses sigma_c. Strip to solid cone changes the falloff from 1/s to 1/s^2. The voices do not change.

Both exits are updates of the store already defined. They are not new sources.
