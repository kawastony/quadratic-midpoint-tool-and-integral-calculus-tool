# Depletion audit

Date: 10 October 2026

Constitution first. On the deep voice the speed increases and that acceleration is the field. On the soft voice the speed is held. The held energy is (1/2) m sigma^2. The integral of acceleration over an interval is the speed change.

## What the constitution gives

From E = (1/2) m sigma^2, the rate is dE/dt = m sigma sigma_dot. On the deep voice sigma_dot = g, so the kinetic energy of the flow rises at m g sigma. That rise is the depletion of the budget that was feeding it. With g = Delta v / T = 0.00160 and m = 1:

n=1: sigma=0.07084, E=0.002509, dE/dt=1.13e-4
n=2: sigma=0.14168, E=0.010037, dE/dt=2.26e-4
n=3: sigma=0.21252, E=0.022582, dE/dt=3.39e-4

The sign is a gain of kinetic energy on the deep voice, which is a depletion of the store that supplied it. Writing sigma_dot = +g is the falling-faster orientation. The opposite sign is the return.

## Where the other answer diverges

The expanding ceiling w_n = w_0 n^p is not in the constitution. It adds a speed w_0 and an exponent p. The substitution E_n = A n^2 (1 + epsilon n^{2-2p}) is correct algebra for that ansatz, and E_3/E_1 = 9 forces epsilon = 0 or p = 1 inside it. But p does not replace w_0. The pure power E_n = E_1 n^{4-2p} is the correction-dominated limit, not the full ansatz. The time law N proportional to t to the power 1/(4-2p) needs constant power, which the constitution does not give, because the power is m g sigma and sigma is changing.

## Stretch with the tools

If the funnel length grows with the voice count and the transit time stays the clock T, the speed required is v_n = n xi / T. At n = 1 that is 0.0113, the local ratio, still below the held 0.07084. It catches the held speed only if the stretch reaches about n = 6. The integral tool tracks this as Delta v = integral a dt over each active interval. No new ceiling is required to write it. A ceiling would be a new scale, and the notes do not contain one.

## Constants

The only universal number the constitution returns is pi, from the two-level half-period. It is a feature of the oscillator, not a new constant, and E/f returns it by construction. xi/T is local. No unit map, no invariant speed, no numerical G, so no Planck length, time, or mass is formable.
