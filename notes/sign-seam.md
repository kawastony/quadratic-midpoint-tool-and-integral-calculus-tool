## Flip

Let x = R/xi and f(x) = ln x - (1 - 1/x). Then f(1) = 0 and f'(x) = (x-1)/x^2 > 0 for x > 1. So the strip drop exceeds the solid drop for every R > xi, and the margin grows without bound. The e in the earlier note is where the strip drop equals g_0 xi, not the crossover. The crossover is at xi. The flip trigger is sigma > sigma_c alone.

## Sigma is a magnitude

The store is the held speed. A speed held by the pause is not a signed charge. Set sigma in [0, infinity) by construction. Then g_0 = sigma / T is nonnegative for every source. Same-sign attraction is that definition, not a second postulate. A negative sigma would be a repulsive sector. Nothing in the constitution or the cone direction selects it. The smallest handoff does not create it.

Lambda is that direction, not a free plus or minus. Flipping it globally has no second universe, because a negative store is not in the model.
