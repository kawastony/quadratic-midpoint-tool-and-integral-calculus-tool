# Two exits for the stretch

Date: 9 October 2026

Constitution first. Equal intervals. Deep voice stretches and adds to the store. Soft voice holds the shape and the speed. Acceptance does not change either.

The cone keeps stretching on each deep voice. The store after n deep voices is sigma = n Delta v. The energy in the stretch is the work of the held force, E = F sigma tau, with F = m g_0 at the source. It accumulates. It does not reverse on the pause.

Two exits, once E passes a threshold E*.

## Return and disperse

The cone springs back to the original shape. The store is not destroyed. It is handed to surrounding cones, one share each. If N neighbors take equal shares,

sigma_neighbor = sigma / N,
g_0,neighbor = sigma_neighbor / T.

The parent is back at sigma = 0 and can stretch again. The process has dispersed. This is the relaxation branch.

## Evolve

The cone does not spring back. The accumulated stretch changes the section. A fixed mouth can open, and a strip can become a solid cone, or the reverse. The active and pause intervals continue. What changes is the falloff: 1/s, 1/s^2, or none, according to the new section. The cycle is the same pair of voices. The geometry is not. The map back to the original cone is the section law and the store, not the silhouette.

Both exits are equivalent at the level of the constitution: equal intervals, a store that only the deep voice fills, a pause that holds, and a receiver that feels the spread field. They differ in whether the parent keeps its shape.
