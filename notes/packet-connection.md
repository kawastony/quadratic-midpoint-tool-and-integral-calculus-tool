## Where it stops

The constitution sets a = g on A. The kink Hamiltonian has no g. Note 00: an acceleration needs a shared clock and a coupling, and neither is in H. Note 17: a sum across A, P_1, and P_2 does not add g, and does not freeze Delta E / d.

The bridge clock used here is v_n = n g tau. The packet clock is T = pi / Delta E. They are not the same time. The table at 9.81 m/s^2 is not the split 0.07084.

So the micro-bridge map shows why the packet looks like the story: two parked pauses, one active transit, a middle that does not move, a transfer that arrives on time. It does not show the packet producing gravity. Setting a = g remains the key added by the constitution, not a term recovered from the walls.
