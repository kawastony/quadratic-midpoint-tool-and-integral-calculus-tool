# One end fixed, and the observer

Date: 11 October 2026

Constitution first. Circumference was fixed. Volume is fixed. The funnel stretches. Equal active and pause. On the deep voice the speed increases. The clock is T = pi / sigma. Time slowed in the active phase is proportional to the gravity strength. The independent observer advises without affecting the dynamics of the active or pause phases, and exit follows acceptance.

## One end fixed

Both ends fixed is a cylinder. Scattering is ill-defined because the optical radius approaches a constant. Fixing only the deep end and letting the far end open, R = R0 + lambda s, restores an impact parameter. The optical geometry is an hourglass with a throat at s* = sqrt(R0 xi / lambda). With R0 = xi = 0.5 and lambda = 1 the throat is at s* = 0.5 and the minimum optical radius is 2. Curvature stays negative. The half-GR deflection returns only if lambda = 1 and xi is identified with GM/c^2. Those are hosted. The spatial slice remains flat.

## Time approaches zero

N = s/(s+xi) goes to 0 as s goes to 0. At s = 0.01, N is 0.02. That is a lapse horizon at the deep end. It is a clock stopping relative to an external parameter, not a signal reaching light speed, and not a depletion of Delta E into external expansion. The constitution does not yet supply an exterior scale factor.

## The observer

The independent observer is defined by not being affected by the active or pause phases. The deep end is where the lapse freezes the clock. That is a candidate place for an observer who does not share the cycle, but the constitution does not place the observer there. The observer is present during both phases and advises without altering the speed or the field. The match is possible, not required. The exit on acceptance is still an external rule, not a consequence of the horizon.
