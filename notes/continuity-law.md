# Continuity law

Date: 9 October 2026

Constitution where it reaches. Own continuity equation where it does not.

## Source

The integral tool says the active interval changes speed by

Delta v = integral_0^{tau} a(t) dt.

The constitution says that active a is the field, and the pause holds speed: a = 0, v+ = v-. So the injected source is that speed change, and the pause does not inject the opposite. Storage along the carrier:

dQ/dt = a(t) on the deep voice,
dQ/dt = 0 on the soft voice.

No separate ratchet. The soft voice is the constitution's hold. Over one pair, Q = Delta v = d Delta E / 2 = 0.07084 at the locked point, and the return half adds nothing.

## Units

Q is a speed. The equal interval tau turns it into an acceleration,

g_source = Q / tau.

Section spreading then multiplies by the area ratio. Solid cone, A(s) = pi sin^2(theta) s^2,

g(s) = (Q / tau) A(xi) / A(s).

Strip, width proportional to s, replaces the area ratio by a width ratio and falls as 1/s. Fixed circumference means A constant, so g(s) = Q/tau with no falloff.

## Test body

The carrier defines Phi by partial_s Phi = -g(s). The test body is given that gradient because the constitution already set the active acceleration equal to the field. Its law on the generator is

d^2 s / dt^2 = g(s)

during the deep voice, and d^2 s / dt^2 = 0 during the soft voice, with speed held. That is the same pair of rules as the packet, applied at s.

## Depletion

Power ledger, own step: the energy spent to keep a mass m speeding up is dE/dt = m a v. With a fixed budget C, the time that budget lasts is C / (m a v). Depletion rate against that budget is inverse to the acceleration cost,

D = C / (m a v),

once v is not zero. Where the cone opens, a = g(s) falls, so the same budget lasts longer. That is the depletion form of the falloff.
