# Alpha dial

Date: 11 October 2026

Constitution first. Volume is exact and unchanging. Circumference is fixed. The funnel stretches. Equal active and pause. Acceleration is the field. The tools track the midpoint identity and the integral of the acceleration.

## What is calculable

The radius law R proportional to Lambda to the -alpha, with alpha from 0 to 1/2, is a calculable interpolation. At alpha = 0 the radius is fixed, the volume grows by Lambda, and the anisotropy is Lambda = 1.5. At alpha = 1/2 the volume is fixed, the radius falls by Lambda to the -1/2, and the anisotropy is Lambda to the 3/2 = 1.837. At alpha = 1/4 both move part way. The ratio of the two endpoint anisotropies is the square root of Lambda = 1.2247. The two readings meet only at Lambda = 1, the pause.

## What the tools do not do

The quadratic midpoint identity is exact for any quadratic. It does not constrain a radius law. The integral tracks the speed change under the field. It does not select alpha. The tools allow both readings. They do not derive the dial that links them.

## Advice

Work with the dial as a bookkeeping device, not as a derived constitution. It makes the conflict visible as one free exponent instead of two substances. It does not remove the conflict. The handoff at Lambda = 1 is forced if both readings are kept, because otherwise the volume runs away. That is a consistency condition, not a new physics. The latent heat is real only if a pressure is supplied, and the constitution does not supply one. The tools can track either reading once it is chosen. They cannot choose it.
