# Drive-topology mismatch

Date: 16 October 2026

## Definition
External progress: packing fraction φ increasing at constant rate.
Internal progress: coordination Z.
Mismatch observable: dZ/dφ.
Lag: φ - φ_int(Z).

## Result on onset model
Under continuous external drive the internal rate dZ/dφ is singular at φc where Z jumps from 0 to Zc. Post-jump growth is continuous. The lag changes sign across the transition.

## Interpretation
Smooth external kinematics and discontinuous internal topology are decoupled. The mismatch peaks at onset. This is a staged constitutive feature, not a derivation of φc.
