# Width robustness

Date: 17 October 2026

## Test
Eight rates. Two smearing kernels: logistic and error function. Width parameter linear in rate. Apparent onset shifted linearly.

## Result
Logistic: FWHM rises then falls at the highest rates (non-monotonic).
Erf: FWHM continues to rise (monotonic within the sampled range).

## Interpretation
The non-monotonic width is kernel-dependent. It is not a robust feature of the present synthetic model. The high-rate outlier under logistic smearing does not appear under erf smearing.

## Status
Non-monotonic broadening is not established. Trend extraction remains valid; the specific non-monotonic claim is not.
