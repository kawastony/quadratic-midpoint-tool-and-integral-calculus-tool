# Variational selection of the transmission profile

Date: 16 October 2026

Minimal effective free-energy scaffold for selecting T(r).

## Strain variable

u(r) = -ln T(r), T(r) = e^{-u(r)}.

## Free-energy functional
F[u] = 4π ∫ r² [ (κ/2)(u')² + V(u) - J(r) u ] dr.

## Euler-Lagrange
κ (u'' + 2/r u') = V'(u) - J(r).

## Weak-field
Quadratic V near u=0 yields screened Poisson. Unscreened limit recovers u ~ 2GM/r, T ~ 1 - 2GM/r.

## Strong-field
Saturating or multi-branch V can produce a jammed plateau u ≈ u_star, T ≈ T_min > 0.

## Status
Effective constitutive selector. Not derived from the locked laws. V(u), J(r), and κ remain inputs. Provides a dynamical route for equilibrium profiles but does not yet close the constitution.
