# Universal gravity, not Earth g

Date: 9 October 2026

Earth's 9.81 m/s^2 is retired for these joins. Universal gravity is Newton's G = 6.67430e-11 m^3 kg^-1 s^-2, so a = G M / r^2. The universal acceleration scale named in the packet notes is a0 = 1.2e-10 m/s^2. The constitution still sets the active acceleration equal to that gravity. Packet point unchanged: Delta E = 0.07084 at v = 1, d = 2, T = 44.35 lattice times, g_c = 0.01771 lattice units.

## Newton

a = G M / r^2. The packet has no mass. Setting r = d times a length and requiring a = a0:

- length unit 1 m, r = 2 m: M = 7.19 kg
- length unit 1 kpc, r = 2 kpc: M = 3.44e9 solar masses

Neither mass is in A(v). A solar mass at 1 kpc gives 1.39e-19 m/s^2, not a0.

## a0 as the active acceleration

v_n = n a0 tau.

Matching a fall speed of 1 m/s after one deep voice forces tau = 8.33e9 s, about 264 years, and still reads the packet's dimensionless 1 as 1 m/s. One transfer clock, if that clock is 1 s, gives v = a0 T = 5.32e-9 m/s. Three voices at tau = 1 s give 3.6e-10 m/s. The packet v remains 1.

## Lattice acceleration to a0

g_c reaches a0 only after multiplication by 6.78e-9. Turning g_c by c^2 / length needs a length of 4.30e5 kpc. Not fixed by the packet.

## Result

Replacing Earth g with G and with a0 removes the local constant. It does not supply the missing mass, length, or time. The active acceleration can be set equal to universal gravity. The wall mass still does not become that acceleration.
