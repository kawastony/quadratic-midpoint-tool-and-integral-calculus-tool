# What sigma is

Date: 9 October 2026

Constitution first. The soft voice holds the speed: v+ = v-. The deep voice is the only interval that increases it. Acceptance does not change the held speed.

Sigma is that held speed. It is the impulse per unit mass the pause refuses to reverse. It is not the potential, and it is not the cone field.

The integral tool fills it. d sigma / dt = lambda a, so after n deep voices

sigma = n Delta v.

At the locked point, one deep voice stores 0.07084 and three store 0.2125. Units: speed.

The source scale is the store divided by the elapsed clock, not the store itself.

g_0 = sigma / (n T) = Delta v / T = 0.00160.

The cone field is that scale spread by section,

g(s) = g_0 A(xi) / A(s).

So the chain is: packet acceleration, gated by the constitution, accumulates as a held speed; the held speed over the clock is the source acceleration; the cone spreads that acceleration into g(s). The receiver feels g(s) only.

Sigma is source memory of speed. Phi, defined by dPhi/ds = -g(s), is the potential the receiver falls in. They are not the same object.
