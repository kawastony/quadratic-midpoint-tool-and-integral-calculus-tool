# Store seam

Date: 10 October 2026

Constitution first. The deep voice increases speed. The soft voice holds it. The integral says the increase is Delta v. Sigma is that held increase, a magnitude.

## Is sigma in the packet?

No. The two-level Hamiltonian is H_2 = (Delta E / 2) sigma_x. Its state is the left/right amplitude. The centroid speed follows from that state and returns to zero over a full clock, because the integral of a(t) over the clock is zero. There is no second variable in H_2 that keeps a speed after the return. Sigma is not a canonical coordinate of the packet. It is the speed the constitution holds outside the packet.

## Conservation split

The active half produces Delta v = d Delta E / 2 = 0.07084. Inside the packet the return half spends that same Delta v, so the packet ends where it started. The store is the copy that left during the feed:

Delta v_active = Delta v_returned + sigma_extracted.

If the return is complete and nothing is extracted, sigma = 0. A nonzero store means the feed took a share before the return. That share is still a definition unless a coupling says how much leaves. The constitution currently takes the whole active Delta v. That is the remaining stipulation.

## Geometric threshold

The cost gap between strip and solid cone, per unit mass, is Delta Phi_strip - Delta Phi_solid. Set the held energy equal to that gap:

(1/2) sigma_c^2 = Delta Phi_strip - Delta Phi_solid.

At R = 4 xi the gap is 5.08e-4, so sigma_c = 0.0319. At R = 8 xi the gap is 9.62e-4, so sigma_c = 0.0439. One deep voice already holds 0.07084, which is above both. So if the threshold is this cost gap, the first deep voice is enough to prefer the solid cone, and sigma_c is fixed by R/xi rather than by hand. The return threshold sigma_r is the same formula at the inner scale where the gap closes, which is xi, where the gap is zero.
