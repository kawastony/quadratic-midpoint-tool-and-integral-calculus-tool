# Two variations

Date: 11 October 2026

Constitution first. Volume is exact and unchanging. Circumference is fixed. The funnel stretches. Equal active and pause. Acceleration is the field.

## The conflict

Fixed circumference, fixed volume, and axial elongation cannot be held at once. Elongate a segment of length h and radius R0 by Lambda = 3/2 while holding R fixed: the volume becomes 1.5 times larger. Hold volume instead: the radius falls as Lambda to the -1/2, and the axial-to-transverse ratio becomes Lambda to the 3/2, which is 1.837, not 1.5. Uniform is over-determined. Non-uniform is under-determined.

## Advice

Run both as parallel readings, not as a single derived connection. The constitution states both constraints. Deriving one from the other would drop a stated exact. The one-end-open form (deep end fixed, far end opens) is a third reading that keeps the deep circumference fixed and lets the far circumference grow. It does not remove the conflict inside a single segment.

## Domains

Fixed circumference is the better reading for the active phase, where the stretch is the field and the transverse scale is held. Fixed volume is the better reading for the pause, where the store is held and the funnel can narrow. Micro (packet, two-level, clock) prefers the fixed-circumference reading, because the clock and the store do not know a changing radius. Macro (funnel, field, receiver) prefers the fixed-volume reading, because continuity and the elongating well need the volume. Pattern propagation and evolution sit between them: the handoff from one cone to its neighbors is where the two readings can meet, one cone holding circumference while the next holds volume, or one end of a single cone holding while the other opens.
