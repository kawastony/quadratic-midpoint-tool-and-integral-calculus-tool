# Effective carrier

Date: 9 October 2026

Status: axiomatic effective law. Not a derivation from the packet alone.

## Two layers

Microscopic packet motion oscillates. Centroid acceleration a(t) = (d Delta E^2 / 2) cos(Delta E t) changes sign. Integral over a full clock is zero.

Effective sourcing keeps the active half and stores it. The pause does not reverse the store. These are constitution rules, not a theorem of the two-level Hamiltonian.

## Axioms

1. Deep voice: the interval where speed is increasing. It sources.
2. Soft voice: the interval where the constitution holds speed. It stores. a = 0 on the carrier.
3. Acceptance does not change the store or the speed.
4. The cone section spreads the stored quantity. It does not create it.

## What is conserved

Not acceleration. The active half delivers an impulse,

J = m Delta v = m integral_0^{tau} a(t) dt.

At the locked point Delta v = 0.07084, so J = 0.07084 m. The pause adds no impulse. The cone spreads force, not acceleration: a solid-cone section A(s) proportional to s^2 gives

F(s) = J / tau * A(xi) / A(s),
g(s) = F(s) / m.

A strip spreads as 1/s. Fixed circumference does not spread.

## Test body

On the deep voice, d^2 s / dt^2 = g(s). On the soft voice, acceleration is zero and speed is held. This is the same pair of axioms at the receiver. It is adopted with the carrier, not derived from the packet geodesic.

## Depletion

Energy spent in an active step is the work of that force, F Delta s. Against a fixed budget C, depletion rate D = C / (F v) once v is not zero. Opening the cone lowers F, so the same budget lasts longer.

## What this is

A piecewise effective theory: the packet supplies the active speed increment, the constitution decides that only that half is stored, the cone spreads the impulse, and a test body follows the same active and pause rules. Coherent as a rulebook. Not forced by the packet Hamiltonian.
