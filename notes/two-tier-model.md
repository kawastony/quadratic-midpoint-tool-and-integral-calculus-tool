# Two-tier model

Date: 16 October 2026

## Tier 1: Constitutive saturation
Variable u, ceiling u★. Finite local capacity. Incremental slope collapses. Smooth plateau. Candidate C.

## Tier 2: Network locking
Variable z (coordination / closure), threshold zc. Rigidity onset when constraints equal degrees of freedom. Discontinuous jump in order parameter.

## Distinction
Filling is not locking. Occupancy and network closure are independent. Granular jamming requires both: pressure can rise continuously while rigidity snaps at isostaticity.

## Status
Tier 1 is locked. Tier 2 is an open extension.
