# Invariant search

Date: 16 October 2026

## Aim
Find a geometric quantity conserved under cone compression that could force the isostatic condition or act as a quantization rule.

## Sweep
Fixed-volume frustum, open radius fixed, tip radius varied. Lateral area, mid-radius, slope, and strain all vary. Volume is constant by construction.

## Result
No additional invariant appears. Nothing in the cone geometry selects Z_c = 2d or supplies a discrete quantization condition. Isostaticity remains an imported constraint-counting result.

## Status
Subtracting method yields no Planck-like constant from the present cone. The geometry organizes; it does not quantize.
