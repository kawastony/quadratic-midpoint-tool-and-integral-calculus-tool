# Axiomatic skeleton

Date: 16 October 2026

## Axiom 1: Kinematic grammar
Six locked laws. Inward cone/funnel under load, shell composition (additive u = -ln T), reciprocal closure, equal active/pause phases, acceleration equal to the field, one-end regularity. Universal form. No hard ceiling.

## Axiom 2: Constitutive capacity
Medium has finite local transmission capacity. Domain 0 ≤ u < u★. Restoring force diverges as u → u★⁻. Not derived from Axiom 1.

## Consequence
Under monotone load the response follows the kinematic branch then crosses into a regime where incremental slope Δu/ΔJ₀ collapses. Only the capacity axiom produces a true plateau.

## Status
Container logic. Analogue systems (granular, traffic, porous) test the constitutive signature. Tunneling and nuclear rates remain outside.
