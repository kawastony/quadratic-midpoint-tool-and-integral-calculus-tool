# Route 1 requirements for a microscopic shell-update law

Date: 16 October 2026

After the packing no-go, saturation is not geometric. Any closure must come from a local constitutive rule.

## Requirements

R1. State variable is u_n = -ln T_n.
R2. Local or nearest-neighbor.
R3. Weak-field linearization yields Poisson-type exterior with u ~ 2GM/r.
R4. Admits u=0 as vacuum (V'(0)=0).
R5. Weak-field stability (V''(0) >= 0).
R6. Permits regular center (u(0) finite, u'(0)=0).
R7. Profiles remain admissible under reciprocal closure.
R8. Strong-field behavior is explicit (stiffening or saturation).
R9. Preferably energy-realizable.
R10. Continuum limit recovers the selector equation.
R11. Minimal.

## Candidates

A. Linear elastic: V = (m^2/2) u^2. Baseline. No saturation.
B. Quartic stiffening: V = (m^2/2) u^2 + (lambda/4) u^4. Recommended next test.
C. Capacity-limited: V with pole at u_star. Effective jammed model, least derived.

## Status

Requirements recorded. Candidate B is the conservative nonlinear extension. Analysis of its regular-center and weak-field behavior is the immediate next calculation.
