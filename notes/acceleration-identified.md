# Acceleration identified

Date: 9 October 2026

Movement: the centroid crosses from -d/2 to +d/2. Speed: v(t) = (d Delta E / 2) sin(Delta E t). The speed changes, so there is an acceleration,

a(t) = dv/dt = (d Delta E^2 / 2) cos(Delta E t).

Locked point Delta E = 0.07084, d = 2. Speed rises from 0 to 0.07084 on the way to the middle, then falls back to 0. Acceleration at the start of the deep voice is 0.00502. It is zero only at the single instant of mid-crossing.

Integral tool: the integral of a from the wall to the middle equals the speed gained, 0.07084. That is the identification. The constitution sets this active acceleration equal to universal gravity.

Midpoint tool: v(t) is a sine, not a quadratic, so the identity is exact only on a short window. At the rising midpoint t = T/8, a window of T/64 recovers the midpoint acceleration to the printed digits. The tool reads the acceleration. It does not replace it.

Energy check: unit-mass kinetic energy rises by (1/2)(0.07084)^2 = 0.00251 on the way in and depletes by the same amount on the way out. Where v is not zero, a = (1/v) dK/dt. The felt increase and the felt depletion are the same acceleration, with opposite sign.

Constitution: the deep voice is the part where this a is nonzero and the speed is changing. That a is gravity. The soft voice is the hold, a = 0, speed kept. Acceptance does not change the value.
