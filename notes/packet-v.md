# Packet v and the tool link

Date: 9 October 2026

The packet has a v. The tool already linked a speed to the acceleration, and the constitution sets that acceleration equal to g.

## Two uses of the letter

Packet v, notes 02 and 10 of grok-notes-version-5: the wall-mass parameter. Localization length xi = 1/(2v). Split Delta E = A(v) exp(-2 v d). Energies scale as v. Lengths scale as 1/v. Any acceleration built from the packet scales as v^2 times a function of v d. At v = 1, d = 2, Delta E = 0.07084.

Tool v: the fall speed. After n deep voices, v_n = n g tau, with a = g on A and a = 0 on the pauses.

## The link already stated

Closing speed sets the flow. Acceleration is the rate of change of that flow, not the closing speed itself. The constitution then sets the active acceleration equal to g. Acceptance does not change either.

## If the packet v is put through that link

Note 10 already builds the only acceleration the packet contains, g_c = Delta E / (2 d). At the earned point that is 0.07084 / 4 = 0.01771 in lattice units. It scales as v^2. Changing the lattice unit multiplies it. It is not 9.81 m/s^2, and it is not independent of d.

Identifying the packet v with the fall speed v_n would make a = g by the constitution, but it imports the unit the Hamiltonian does not have. Note 10 names the missing imports: a length, hbar, and a field coupling. None is fixed by A(v).

So the v is there, and the tool does connect a speed to an acceleration. The packet v is the wall mass. The tool v is the stacked fall. Setting them equal is the joint still not written.
