# Inverse observer

Date: 14 October 2026

The inverse relation between time and space is the reciprocal closure: g_tt = -T, g_rr = T^{-1}. The observer outside sees the clock inside slowed. The person inside falls in the elongating space. The outside observer does not see the fall as motion of the exterior. Both are consistent with the product of the two factors remaining unity. That is the equivalence principle in the lattice language.
