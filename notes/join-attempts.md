# Attempts to join packet v to g

Date: 9 October 2026

Constitution: a = g on the deep voice, a = 0 on the pause, v+ = v-, equal interval tau. Tool: midpoint identity, closing speed sets the flow, acceleration is the rate of change of the flow. Packet: v is the wall mass, Delta E = 0.07084 at v = 1, d = 2, T = pi / Delta E.

The pure overlap formula A(1) exp(-4) gives 0.03663, not the locked numerical split. The attempts use the locked 0.07084.

## 1. Packet v is the fall speed

v_n = n g tau. Setting v_1 equal to the packet value 1, with g = 9.81 m/s^2, forces tau = 0.102 s and reads the 1 as 1 m/s. The packet 1 is dimensionless. The second is imported.

## 2. Packet v is the closing speed, and a = u / tau

The tool says closing speed sets the flow and a is the change in that flow. Setting u = 1 and a = g again forces tau = u/g = 0.102 s. Same import.

## 3. Convective acceleration a = u^2 / d

With u = v = 1 and d = 2, a = 0.5 in lattice units. Not g, and not independent of d.

## 4. The packet's own acceleration g_c = Delta E / (2 d)

0.07084 / 4 = 0.01771 lattice units. Reaching 9.81 multiplies by 554. Reaching 1.2e-10 multiplies by 6.8e-9. Note 10 already marks that multiplier as a fit, not a derivation. If the lattice acceleration is turned by c^2 / length, the length that yields 9.81 m/s^2 is about 8.4e13 m. The length that yields 1.2e-10 m/s^2 is about 2.2e5 kpc. Neither is fixed by A(v).

## 5. Midpoint tool on the split

The split is exponential in d, not quadratic. At separation 2 and half-width 1, the secant is 1.81 times the midpoint slope. At half-width 0.1 the ratio is 1.007. The identity is the small-gap limit. It does not hold on the packet interval, so it does not convert Delta E into g.

## 6. Transfer clock as the equal interval

T = pi / 0.07084 = 44.35 lattice times. One deep voice at a = g then gives v = g T = 435 m/s if the lattice time is 1 s. The packet v is 1. The arrival speed d/T is 0.045. None of the three numbers agree.

## 7. Fixed-arrival funnel

Speed L/T with L = d and T the transfer clock is 0.045 lattice units. Specific kinetic energy is 0.001. The constitution energy grows as L^2 only after L is a length and T is the voice interval. The packet does not hold volume and circumference fixed, so the funnel integral does not apply to it.

## Result

Seven joins were written. Each either imports a unit, fails the midpoint identity, or returns a number that is not g and still depends on d. The constitution can set a = g after a speed exists. It cannot turn the wall mass into that speed.
