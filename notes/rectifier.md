# Rectifier

Date: 9 October 2026

Constitution first. Integral tool second. Independent store equation where the packet does not supply one.

## Switch

The deep voice is the interval where the boundaries are closing and the speed is increasing. The soft voice is the hold. Encode that as a switch lambda equal to 1 on the deep voice and 0 on the soft voice. Acceptance does not flip lambda.

## Store

The carrier has its own state sigma, a stored speed increment. The integral tool supplies the gain. The switch supplies the gate.

d sigma / dt = lambda(t) a(t).

On the deep voice this integrates to Delta v. On the soft voice the derivative is zero, so the return half of the packet cosine does not propagate. After n deep voices, sigma = n Delta v. At the locked point, three deep voices store 0.2125. The store does not reverse.

## Two accelerations

Source scale, from the rectified clock average:

g_0 = Delta v / T = 0.00160

in packet units. Field on the solid cone, after the store is spread by area:

g(s) = g_0 A(xi) / A(s) = g_0 (xi / s)^2.

At s = xi, 2 xi, 4 xi, 8 xi the field is 0.00160, 0.000399, 0.0000998, 0.0000250. A strip uses width instead of area and falls as 1/s. Fixed circumference does not fall.

## Receiver

Unchanged from the potential action. The test body feels g(s), not lambda. The switch is on the source.

## Status

The rectifier is now an equation for a carrier state, gated by the constitution and filled by the integral. It is still not a mode of the two-level Hamiltonian. It is the hardware the effective model was missing.
