## Gravity gates

No single reading passes both a Newtonian gate and a GR-like clock gate.

The feed is a point coupling, so there is no arrival and no bending. The framework is a force on a medium, not a metric.

## Triangle

At the first voice the two differ by 2/sigma = 28.2. Squaring the hypotenuse gives a parabola, not E^2 = (pc)^2 + (mc^2)^2.

It dies as sigma to 0, so it is a correction, not a leg. An ambient field would supply a real leg, but it is imported. A geometric floor with the only length in the notes is order 1 and would dominate the spectrum.
