# Granular fit results v1

Date: 16 October 2026

## Dataset
Synthetic data generated from published O’Hern / Majmudar scalings:
- φc = 0.64
- Zc = 6
- Z - Zc ∝ (φ - φc)^0.5
- P ∝ (φ - φc)^1.0 (harmonic)

## Tier 1 fit
Saturating form u = u★ (1 - exp(-Δφ / Jc))
Fitted: u★ ≈ 0.116, Jc ≈ 0.038
RMSE ≈ 0.0068 on the synthetic pressure curve.

## Tier 2
Coordination follows the published square-root rise above Zc. Locking threshold is the isostatic value itself.

## Interpretation
Tier 1 reproduces a continuous pressure-like response. Tier 2 is required for the discontinuous onset in Z. The two-tier split organizes the known signatures. Coupling between u and z remains a free parameter.
